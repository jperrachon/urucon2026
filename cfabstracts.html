Categories

Submission Categories

Authors must select the category that best fits their submission at the time of upload.

Student
Intended for preliminary work by students that has not yet reached the maturity or scope required for a full paper submission. It is a space to share work in progress, early results, or thesis-related explorations, and to receive feedback from the academic and professional community.
Researcher
Intended for recent research work that authors wish to present at the conference without going through the full paper submission and review process. Well suited for sharing recently published results, ongoing projects, or work that authors prefer to disseminate in shorter form.
Industry / Professional
Intended for technological developments from the industrial sector or professionals. Accommodates both work that has not yet reached the maturity of a full paper, and work whose authors prefer to participate without committing to a full paper submission. Submissions in this category are expected to highlight applied or practical contributions.

Note: This conference accepts technological developments in the Call for Papers track as well.
Preparation

Abstract Preparation

Submitted abstracts must include all of the following elements:

1
Title
A clear and descriptive title for your submission.
2
Authors and affiliations
Full list of authors and their institutional affiliations.
3
Abstract text (max. 1000 words)
Abstract text of no more than 1000 words, excluding references.
4
References
References included separately and not counted in the word limit.
5
Selected category
Select the appropriate category: Student, Researcher, or Industry / Professional.
6
Presentation preference
Authors' preference between oral and poster presentation. The final decision will be made by the Technical Committee based on the program structure and the nature of the contribution.
Evaluation

Submission & Evaluation

Acceptance
Abstract meets the requirements for the selected category and is accepted for presentation.
Rejection
Abstract does not meet the standards for the selected category.
Abstracts will be reviewed by members of the Technical Committee. Each submission will be evaluated according to its category. Authors will be asked to indicate their preference between oral and poster presentation; however, the final decision on the presentation format will be made by the Technical Committee.
Certificates

Certificates for Authors & Presenters

Certificate of presentation
Each author or presenter of an accepted abstract will receive a certificate of presentation. The certificate will include the authors' names and the abstract title.
