Submission Tracks

Call for Papers
Peer-reviewed · IEEE Xplore indexed

Peer-reviewed track (3 reviewers, single-blind) following IEEE Standards. Accepted papers will be published and indexed in IEEE Xplore. Only English papers are accepted.

  • Double-column A4 IEEE Conference Template
  • Maximum 5 pages including references
  • Upload via EDAS: edas.info/N35083
  • Author Registration fee required for publication
  • Single-blind review by 3 external reviewers
Full instructions
Call for Abstracts
Book of Abstracts · No IEEE Xplore indexing

Abstracts evaluated by the Technical Committee. English and Spanish accepted (English preferred). Accepted abstracts will be compiled in a Conference Book of Abstracts.

  • Three categories: Student, Researcher, Industry/Professional
  • Maximum 1000 words (excluding references)
  • Upload via Google Forms (link on CfA page)
  • Oral or poster presentation at TC's decision
  • Certificate of presentation for accepted authors
